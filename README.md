Introduction

EyeOnCash: Expense Tracker is a personal finance management application published on Google Play by Younestec.

EyeOnCash: Expense Tracker helps users manage income, expenses, budgets, subscriptions, credit cards, and financial accounts. We value your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal information.

Information We Collect

EyeOnCash: Expense Tracker is designed to minimize data collection.

The app displays Google AdMob banner advertisements on the Home screen. The advertising SDK collects and shares technical data described in the Advertising section below.

The application may process financial information entered by the user, such as accounts, transactions, budgets, subscriptions, credit cards, categories, and related app settings.

Google account information is used only when the user chooses to sign in for Google Drive backup and restore.

EyeOnCash: Expense Tracker does not require users to sign in to use the application.

Google Sign-In

Google Sign-In is completely optional.

If you choose to sign in with your Google Account, it is used only to:

- Back up your data to your own Google Drive.
- Restore your backup from Google Drive.

Your Google account is not used for advertising or marketing purposes.

Data Storage

Your financial data is primarily stored locally on your device.

If you choose to use Google Drive Backup, your backup is stored in your own Google Drive account.

Google Drive Backup

If backup is enabled, EyeOnCash: Expense Tracker may create a backup file in your Google Drive app data folder. This backup is used only to help you restore your own app data.

Data Sharing

Google AdMob collects and shares technical advertising data, including IP addresses, app interactions, diagnostics and device or app identifiers, for advertising, analytics, security and fraud prevention. Google processes this data under its privacy policies.

Financial records and backup contents are used for the app's finance and backup features. The app's ad requests contain no transaction amounts, balances, account details, card information, financial notes or backup contents.

Advertising

EyeOnCash: Expense Tracker uses Google AdMob to display banner advertisements. AdMob may collect an IP address (which can indicate approximate location), interactions with the app and advertisements, diagnostic information, and device or app identifiers such as App Set ID. The current Android build removes the advertising ID permission; other SDK identifiers can still be processed.

This technical data is used for advertising or marketing, analytics, and fraud prevention, security and compliance. Google may process this data on its servers and share it with advertising partners according to its policies. See [Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy) and [How Google uses information from apps](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites).

Where applicable, Google's User Messaging Platform presents privacy messages and records your choices before ad requests. When privacy options are required, you can revisit them in Settings > Ad privacy options. Availability and personalization of ads depend on your choices, region and applicable requirements.

Google account information used for optional sign-in and backup is kept separate from the app's advertising requests.

Data Security

Reasonable security measures are implemented to help protect your information. However, no method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure.

Children’s Privacy

EyeOnCash: Expense Tracker is not intended for children under the age of 13.

Data Deletion

To request deletion of your EyeOnCash authentication account and associated developer-held data, visit [Request account and data deletion](https://mohamedyounes91.github.io/masarify-privacy/delete-account.html). This page is also linked from Settings > Request account deletion in the updated app.

Send a request to support.masarify@gmail.com from the Google email address used for EyeOnCash, with the subject "Delete EyeOnCash account". We may ask for information needed to verify account ownership. Support reviews the request and deletes the associated EyeOnCash authentication account and developer-held account data. We explain any additional verification or processing steps in our reply.

Local financial records remain under your control on your device. You can remove them using Settings > Reset local data. Backups stored in your own Google Drive remain under your control; you can delete the app's hidden backup data through Google Drive > Settings > Manage apps > EyeOnCash > Options > Delete hidden app data, where available. Contact support for assistance if the app is not listed.

Deleting the EyeOnCash account concerns the account used by this app. Your Google account and unrelated Google Drive files remain under your control.

Google controls retention of advertising data it receives. See Google's privacy and data-control information linked above for those practices.
