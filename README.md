Privacy Policy for Masarify
Last updated: August 2026
1. Introduction
Masarify is a personal finance management application designed to help users manage their income, expenses, budgets, subscriptions, and financial accounts.
We value your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal information.
2. Information We Collect
Masarify is designed to minimize data collection.
The application may process:
Financial information entered by the user (such as accounts, transactions, budgets, subscriptions, and categories).
Optional Google account information only when the user chooses to sign in for Google Drive backup and restore.
Masarify does not require users to sign in to use the application.
3. Google Sign-In
Google Sign-In is completely optional.
If you choose to sign in with your Google Account, it is used only to:
Back up your data to your own Google Drive.
Restore your backup from Google Drive.
Your Google account is not used for advertising or marketing purposes.
4. Data Storage
Your financial data is primarily stored locally on your device.
If you choose to use Google Drive Backup, your backup is stored in your own Google Drive account.
5. Data Sharing
Masarify does not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties for advertising purposes.
6. Advertising
Currently, Masarify does not display third-party advertisements.
If advertising is introduced in a future version, this Privacy Policy will be updated before those changes take effect.
7. Data Security
Reasonable security measures are implemented to help protect your information.
However, no method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure.
8. Children's Privacy
Masarify is not intended for children under the age of 13.
9. Changes to This Privacy Policy
This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time.
Any changes will be reflected by updating the "Last updated" date at the top of this page.
